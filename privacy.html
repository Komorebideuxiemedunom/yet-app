Privacy Policy

YET (bucket list app) · effective September 29, 2026

In short

Your list, memories and photos stay on your iPhone and, if you turn it on, in your own iCloud. YET has no user accounts and sends nothing to servers owned by the app's publisher.

What the app stores

  • Your dreams, plans, memories, photos and settings: on your iPhone, and in your Apple account's private iCloud database if iCloud sync is on. The publisher cannot access them.
  • AI API keys you enter: in the iPhone keychain, never sent to the publisher.

Permissions (all optional)

  • Approximate location: for weather, sun times and finding real places around you (Apple Maps). The app doesn't store or share it; place searches and weather are handled by Apple (Maps, Weather) under their own terms.
  • Calendar: read on the iPhone to find free time. Nothing is sent or changed.
  • Notifications: reminders and motivational messages, scheduled on the iPhone.
  • Photos: only the ones you pick for a memory are copied into the app.

Artificial intelligence

  • Apple Intelligence (compatible iPhones): runs on the iPhone.
  • AI with your own key (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Mistral, Groq, Grok, DeepSeek, OpenRouter): only if you add a key. Your questions to Sol and the context they need (your dreams, approximate city, weather) are then sent directly to the provider you chose, under its own terms. The publisher never sees them.

Sharing

The publisher does not sell, rent or share any data. Nothing is collected for advertising in this version.

Your rights

You can delete everything at any time: delete dreams in the app, delete the app, and manage iCloud data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud. For questions, see the support page.

Changes

This policy will be updated before any change in how data is used (for example ads in the free version), and the date above will change.
